## 4. What this establishes

- **R-2 refusal on the current runtime:** an altered local declaration under
  verified `AUTHORIZED` availability is refused as
  `LOCAL_DECLARATION_TAMPERED`, with the target unchanged and the refusal
  delivered to the model as a host error. This holds in 4 of 4 runs under
  `b0dc1a14`, on Claude Code 2.1.303 headless.
- **Controls under the same pins:**
  - P-1: the AUTHORIZED path does not over-refuse.
  - P-3: without a declaration, a violating edit is still blocked by the
    architectural check.
  - P-4: without a declaration, a missing memory does not trigger an
    availability refusal.

## 6. Findings for review

- **E4 traffic outside R-2:** `api.github.com` appears once in P-3 (Write 1)
  and once in P-4 (Edit 1). P-3 and P-4 make no verifier call: the wrapper
  shows no verification. From the available evidence, this traffic is not
  attributable to the Mneme verifier. P-1, P-3 and P-4 do not require E4
  (`e4=False`); they were captured only for uniformity.
- **E4 traffic in R-2:** `github.com` (the verifier's fixture read, matching
  the wrapper's endpoint) appears in all four R-2 captures. `api.anthropic.com`
  appears in Edit 2, and it is not attributable to the verifier from the
  available evidence. No forbidden host appears in any capture.
