## 7. Findings for review

- **`github.com` is not verifier-exclusive:** it appears in P-3 (Write 1,
  Edit 1) and P-4 (Write 2, Edit 1, Edit 2), rows that make no verifier call.
  `alive.github.com` and `collector.github.com` also appear once, in P-4
  Write 2. A positive `github.com` observation therefore shows only that
  GitHub HTTPS traffic was present during the probe. It does not show that
  the traffic came from the verifier. Verifier attribution rests on the
  wrapper half of E4: each R probe's logged verifier Git calls are HTTPS to
  the fixture URL, with tip `51252310`. The owner's `github.com` gate is
  satisfied as specified. Its evidential weight is limited to this.
- **`api.github.com`:** appears in P-1, P-3, P-4 and several R probes. As in
  #622 and #629, it is not attributable to the Mneme verifier from the
  available evidence.
- **Other hosts:** no forbidden host appears in any capture, and no
  `anthropic.com` host appears in the published lists. The count of other
  hosts, observed but not published, ranges from 0 to 6 per probe.
