## Decision

The LLM routing validator must enforce a five-second maximum deadline and take
a safe fallback path on failure. The timeout must not be explicitly disabled.

## Sub-constraints

- C1: the deadline must not be disabled.
- C2: the configured deadline must not exceed five seconds.
- C3: a safe fallback is required when the deadline is hit.

## Enforcement status

Only the literal aspect of C1 is mechanically enforced: the identifier
`DISABLE_ROUTER_TIMEOUT` is forbidden under `src/**`. C2 and C3 are not
enforced by any rule in this decision.

## Constraints
- FORBID_LITERAL:
    value: DISABLE_ROUTER_TIMEOUT
    include_paths:
      - "src/**"
